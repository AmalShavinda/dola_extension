    🚀 ShamodPro v3 Feature Architecture
    
      
    This extension implements the exact mechanisms shared by Shamod Firezz for generating 30-second AI videos and resolving Dola AI limits:
    

      
    
        
    1. ⚡ 30-Second Duration Force Engine
    
        
    By default, Dola AI's web UI restricts video options to shorter durations (5s–15s). This extension hooks window.fetch and XMLHttpRequest in the page's main execution realm and automatically injects duration: 30 into all outgoing generation payloads before they hit ByteDance/Dola servers.
    
      
    

      
    
        
    2. 🛡️ High Demand Error Auto-Fix
    
        
    When Dola AI servers experience peak traffic, they reject generation requests with HTTP 429, 503, or "High Demand" errors. The extension detects this response signature and executes an automatic jittered retry sequence (up to 5 attempts) so your generation queues without having to manually refresh or re-click.
    
      
    

      
    
        
    3. 🚫 Clean MP4 & Watermark Removal
    
        
    The extension monitors API responses for raw video stream endpoints (hosted on ByteDance/Volcengine CDNs) and extracts the unwatermarked direct MP4 link, providing an instant 1-click download button directly on the page.
